📱 Install NOGAFA on your iPhone

  1. Tap the Share button ⎋ at the bottom of Safari
  2. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen
  3. Tap Add — the NOGAFA icon appears on your home screen

⚙️ One-time setup needed

Open index.html and paste your Apps Script web-app URL (the one ending in /exec) into the APP_URL line near the bottom of the file.

📡 No internet connection

NOGAFA needs an internet connection to open your live financial records. Connect and try again.
